# Sholu

Content reference: `/Users/bayudf/Documents/GitHub/SalatBuddy`. See `CONTENT_SOURCES.md` for evidence behind the website copy.

Sholu is an iPhone salah companion for people learning to pray, especially new Muslims. It guides movements and recitations in real time. The visitor can explore the original app screens and watch the owner-supplied demo.

Personalization starts with movement knowledge. A beginner goes directly to stage 1; other users continue to questions about Al-Fatihah, Final Tashahhud, and other recitations. The starting stage uses beginner status and Al-Fatihah/Final Tashahhud fluency. Stage 1 uses tasbih while standing and during Tashahhud; stage 2 includes Al-Fatihah and Final Tashahhud; stage 3 includes all recitations. Do not imply that every user receives every question or all recitations.

With microphone access enabled, the app listens to recitations and guides the next step. Reflection is optional and rates fluency from 1 to 5 using questions for the prayer's stage. Recorded reflections can make the user eligible for an offer of longer passages or the next stage. The user can accept or retain the same guidance. Do not promise automatic advancement or an offer after every prayer.
